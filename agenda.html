Task categories

Groups the Today list into collapsible sections — handy for tucking away routine/chore tasks when you're busy.

Skincare

Your steps, in the order you use them. Retire a product to take it off the daily line but keep its history — that's the move for a finished bottle. Delete is for typos.

Show on the day view
The one quiet line under your meals. Turning this off hides it completely — your products and history stay put.
Exercises

The exercises you can log, and which template each one uses — lifting asks for weight, reps and sets; cardio asks for time and distance. Retire one to take it off the day view but keep its history. Delete is for typos; logged entries keep the name they were logged under either way.

Units
What new entries are logged in. Entries you've already logged keep the unit they were logged with.
